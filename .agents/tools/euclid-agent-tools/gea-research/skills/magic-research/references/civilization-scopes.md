# Civilization-Period Scopes

## Ancient Core (Primary Focus)

### 1. Mesopotamia
| Period | Date Range | Key Sites | Known Geometric Baseline |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|
| Ubaid | 6500–3800 BCE | Eridu, Ur, Tell al-'Ubaid | Painted pottery with geometric bands, early temple platforms |
| Uruk | 3800–3200 BCE | Uruk (Warka), Susa | Cylinder seals, monumental architecture, proto-writing, cone mosaics |
| Early Dynastic | 2900–2350 BCE | Ur, Lagash, Nippur, Kish | Royal tombs, elaborate seals, votive statues, Standard of Ur |
| Akkadian | 2350–2150 BCE | Akkad, Sippar, Tell Brak | Naturalistic seal art, Naram-Sin stele, imperial iconography |
| Ur III | 2112–2004 BCE | Ur, Nippur, Drehem | Ziggurats, standardized administration, mathematical tablets |
| Old Babylonian | 2000–1600 BCE | Babylon, Mari, Larsa | Hammurabi stele, mathematical peak (Plimpton 322), Ishtar cult |
| Kassite | 1600–1155 BCE | Dur-Kurigalzu, Nippur | Kudurru stones, modular brick architecture |
| Neo-Assyrian | 911–609 BCE | Nineveh, Nimrud, Khorsabad | Palace reliefs, lamassu, astronomical records |
| Neo-Babylonian | 626–539 BCE | Babylon | Ishtar Gate, Processional Way, glazed brick technology peak |

### 2. Egypt
| Period | Date Range | Key Sites | Known Geometric Baseline |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|
| Predynastic | 5500–3100 BCE | Naqada, Hierakonpolis | Painted pottery, palettes, early symbolic motifs |
| Old Kingdom | 2686–2181 BCE | Giza, Saqqara, Dahshur | Pyramids, mathematical papyri origins, grid-based art |
| Middle Kingdom | 2055–1650 BCE | Thebes, Lahun, Lisht | Refined proportion systems, mathematical papyri (Rhind, Moscow) |
| New Kingdom | 1550–1069 BCE | Thebes, Amarna, Abu Simbel | Temple complexes, tomb painting, Amarna artistic revolution |
| Late Period | 664–332 BCE | Saïs, Memphis | Greek influence, synthesis period |

### 3. Greece
| Period | Date Range | Key Sites | Known Geometric Baseline |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|
| Geometric | 900–700 BCE | Athens, Argos | Geometric pottery style, meander patterns, abstract figuration |
| Archaic | 700–480 BCE | Athens, Corinth, Ionia | Column orders, kouros proportions, black-figure pottery |
| Classical | 480–323 BCE | Athens, Olympia, Delphi | Parthenon, golden ratio, Euclidean geometry, red-figure pottery |
| Hellenistic | 323–31 BCE | Alexandria, Pergamon, Antioch | Archimedean geometry, complex mosaics, architectural innovation |

### 4. Rome

## Museum API Priority Order

For all civilizations, search in this order:
1. **Metropolitan Museum of Art** — met.museum API, broad collection
2. **British Museum** — britishmuseum.org collection online
3. **Louvre** — collections.louvre.fr
4. **Smithsonian** — si.edu APIs
5. **Wikimedia Commons** — commons.wikimedia.org
6. **Civilization-specific museums** (e.g., Iraq Museum, Egyptian Museum Cairo, National Museum Delhi)
7. **University collections** (Penn Museum, Yale Babylonian Collection, Oriental Institute Chicago)

## Key Search Pattern

For any civilization-period-element combination:
```
BROAD (start here):     "[CIVILIZATION] [ELEMENT]"
NARROW (refine):        "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] [ELEMENT] [CARRIER]"
MUSEUM (source):        "[ELEMENT] [CIVILIZATION] museum collection"
ACADEMIC (verify):      "[ELEMENT] [CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] archaeological evidence"
```
