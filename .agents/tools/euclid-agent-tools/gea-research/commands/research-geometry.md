## Core Question
What GEA/GEM compositions are detectable in the material record? What dimensional range? What is the spatial sophistication baseline at this moment? What construction sequences are in use?

## Research Probes — Execute Each

### Probe 1: GEA Inventory
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] geometric patterns", "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] geometric motifs survey"
Find: What atomic geometric primitives (circle, line, triangle, arc, angle, square, spiral, point) are in active use? Which well-established vs. newly appearing?

### Probe 2: GEM Compositions
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] composite geometric design", "[ELEMENT] [CIVILIZATION] composition analysis"
Find: What molecular compositions achieved? What interaction rules between GEAs being exploited? (Two overlapping squares rotated 45° = 8-star. Circle + radial lines = rosette. Triangle + repeated reflection = tessellation.)

### Probe 3: Dimensional Range
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] relief sculpture technique", "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] architectural geometry"
Find: What dimensional rendering is current? 1D (incised), 2D (painted/relief), 3D (sculptural/architectural)? Is the notch showing dimensional EXPANSION?

### Probe 4: GEA/GEM Decomposition
Search: "[ELEMENT] geometric construction analysis", "[ELEMENT] symmetry group properties"
Find: For each candidate — what is the full atomic-to-molecular construction chain? How many GEA components? What compositional rules? What construction sequence produces the GEM from constituent GEAs?

### Probe 5: Carrier Constraints
Search: "[ELEMENT] [CARRIER_TYPE] geometric constraints", "[CIVILIZATION] [CARRIER_TYPE] geometric technique"
Find: How does carrier dimensionality constrain the GEA/GEM? Same rosette on flat tablet vs. curved cylinder seal vs. glazed brick requires different construction intelligence.

### Probe 6: G(t) Baseline Assessment
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] geometric development [PERIOD] survey", "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] spatial complexity"
Find: Where does this notch sit on the civilization's spatial sophistication trajectory? GEA range, GEM complexity, GEK-T tier max, dimensional range. Is G(t) accelerating, stable, or stagnating?

## Decomposition Protocol
For EVERY GEM encountered, execute the full decomposition:
1. Identify constituent GEAs

4. Identify carrier constraints
5. Identify GEK-T tier
6. Identify construction sequence
