# Grade-Level Calibration Reference

The 15-section architecture is grade-invariant. These dimensions scale with grade:

## Vocabulary

| Concept | Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Symmetry | "It looks the same 8 different ways" | "8-fold symmetry" | "D₈ symmetry group with 16 isometries" |
| GEA/GEM | "This shape is made of two squares" | "Two overlapping squares at 45°" | "GEM.8star(GEA.square×2, rotation=45°)" |
| Affordance | "Your eye travels equally" | "Equal visual weight in all directions" | "Radial equilibrium creates perceptual completeness" |
| Power | "Who got to use this symbol?" | "Who was allowed to wear this, and who wasn't?" | "What class structures determined access to this iconography?" |

## Mathematical Depth (B2)

| Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Count sides, count angles | Measure angles, identify properties | Name properties formally |
| "How many lines of symmetry?" | "What stays the same when you rotate?" | "The symmetry group has N elements because..." |
| Use rulers, count | Use protractors, calculate | Approach proof, use notation |

## Critical Analysis (A4, B4)

| Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| "Who got to use this?" | "Why could priests but not farmers use it?" | "What economic structures determined access?" |
| Binary access (yes/no) | Graduated access (why) | Systemic analysis (structures) |

## Construction Precision (A6, B7)

| Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Simple compass-and-straightedge | Precise compass work, measured angles | Engineering models, tolerance specification |
| "Make a circle" | "Make a circle with radius 5cm" | "Construct to within 1mm tolerance" |

## Elaboration Range (A2c)

| Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Stay within civilization | Bridge to 1-2 other civilizations | Cross-cultural, cross-temporal comparison |
