## Core Question
What mathematical knowledge exists at this notch? What has been proven, calculated, measured, or standardized? What functional capabilities does this enable? And critically — what is PRACTICED but not yet formalized (the formalization gap)?

## Research Probes — Execute Each

### Probe 1: Formalized Knowledge
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] mathematics tablets calculations", "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] mathematical text"
Find: What mathematical tablets, calculation tables, or pedagogical texts exist? What properties, formulas, constants, or methods are explicitly recorded? (π approximations, Pythagorean triples, area calculations, division tables.)

### Probe 2: GEK Identification
Search: "[ELEMENT] mathematical properties ancient", "[ELEMENT] geometric properties formal"
Find: For each candidate element — what GEK (conceptual/functional truth) does it embody? What is mathematically TRUE about this GEA/GEM that was always true but is being RECOGNIZED at this notch? Raw material for B2.

### Probe 3: GEK-T Operations
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] geometric transformation", "[ELEMENT] rotation reflection symmetry ancient"
Find: What transformations are being performed? Rotation, reflection, scaling, projection, tessellation, extrusion? What GEK-T tier achieved — single operation (Tier 1), combined (Tier 2), chained with feedback (Tier 3)?

### Probe 4: Functional Applications
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] astronomy mathematical", "[CIVILIZATION] engineering [PERIOD] construction technique", "[ELEMENT] functional application ancient [CIVILIZATION]"
Find: Where are mathematical properties DEPLOYED? Astronomy, surveying, construction, irrigation, navigation, timekeeping, trade? Each deployment = potential B5 invention candidate.

### Probe 5: The Formalization Gap
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] craft knowledge empirical", "[CIVILIZATION] artisan mathematics implicit"
Find: What do artisans' hands know that scribes' tablets don't record? Where does G(t) exceed M(t)? This gap is one of the most pedagogically powerful findings — reveals the liminal skill pathway.

### Probe 6: STEM Notch Candidates
Search: "[CIVILIZATION] [PERIOD] invention technology", "[INVENTION_CANDIDATE] archaeological analysis mechanism"
Find: Which functional stabilizations (inventions) deploy the geometric properties? Full decomposition: GEK × GEK-T × GEA/GEM → invention. What GEK-T tier? What prerequisites?
