## Recommended Perplexity Search Strategy

For each artifact:
1. **Start broad**: "Mesopotamian Ishtar 8-pointed star"
2. **Add specifics**: "British Museum collection high resolution"
3. **Include context**: "glazed brick relief Pergamon museum"
4. **Seek primary sources**: "archaeological photograph museum catalog"
5. **Look for academic**: "Louvre Oriental Institute Chicago"

Key museums to mention in searches:
- British Museum (London)
- Pergamon Museum (Berlin)
- Louvre (Paris)
- Oriental Institute (Chicago)
- Iraq Museum (Baghdad)
- Yale Babylonian Collection
- Metropolitan Museum of Art (NYC)
